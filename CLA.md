# Individual Contributor License Agreement

> **Draft — not legal advice.** Have a lawyer review this before asking anyone to sign it.

## In plain words

- **You keep your copyright.** This is a licence, not a transfer.
- **The project may use your contribution under AGPL-3.0 and under other licences,**
  including commercial ones. This is what lets the copyright holder offer the software
  under other terms (see [`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md)) without asking every contributor.
- **The project promises your contribution stays open.** Whatever else happens, it remains
  available under AGPL-3.0 or a later version (section 4).
- **You confirm it's yours to give**, and that your employer or school doesn't own it (or
  has agreed).

To sign, comment on your pull request:

```
I have read the CLA in CLA.md and I agree to it.
```

The maintainer records your GitHub username in [`docs/cla-signatures.md`](docs/cla-signatures.md).
You sign once; it covers all your future contributions.
